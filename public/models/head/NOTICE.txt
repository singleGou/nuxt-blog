The original skin model was created from Visible Human Male data provided by
the U.S. National Library of Medicine. Original metadata lists Kristen Browne
and Heidi Schlehlein as creators.

Changes by HYY Blog:
- Clipped the skin at Y=0.685 metres in the source coordinate system to retain
  the head and upper neck only. Triangles crossing that plane were clipped;
  the neck opening is left uncapped. Torso and limbs are not included.
- Discarded vertex colors and adjusted the material to a transparent, rim-emphasized
  head contour. No face reconstruction or independent head rescaling was applied.
- Used exactly the translation and uniform scaling recorded in the brain asset's
  provenance.json, retaining the source HRA alignment between brain and skin.

This is a skin outline for spatial context, not a skull layer or a complete head
anatomy atlas. Brain geometry comes from a separate HRA reference organ; see
/models/brain/NOTICE.txt. Both reference organs share the HRA body coordinate system.

Rebuild with Node 22: node scripts/prepare-head-model.mjs [cached-VH_M_Skin.glb]
Source/output checksums and extraction parameters are recorded in provenance.json.
