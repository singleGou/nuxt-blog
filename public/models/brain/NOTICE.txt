Brain model attribution
=======================
Kristen Browne; Heidi Schlehlein. 2023.
3D Reference Organ for Brain, Male v1.3. HuBMAP Human Reference Atlas.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.48539/HBM929.XKCL.339
Source: https://purl.humanatlas.io/ref-organ/brain-male/v1.3
Asset: https://cdn.humanatlas.io/digital-objects/ref-organ/brain-male/v1.3/assets/3d-vh-m-allen-brain.glb
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Source provenance: Allen Human Reference Atlas - 3D, 2020 (Ding et al., 2016).
The source atlas mirrored 141 structures to form a whole brain and resized them
to the Visible Human Male body. It is not a subject-specific scan of two hemispheres.
HRA entry and original model metadata supply the above attribution and license.

Changes by HYY Blog:
- Centered and uniformly rescaled the geometry, preserving relative positions.
- Quantized positions to signed 16-bit and normals to signed 8-bit, using the
  standard glTF KHR_mesh_quantization extension. Topology and all 283 meshes retained.
- Replaced original colors with a neutral material and interactive highlights.
- Grouped selected named structures into six introductory educational entries.
  The prefrontal entry highlights related frontal gyri, not a precise prefrontal
  boundary; superior/middle frontal gyri extend beyond the prefrontal cortex.
  No additional anatomical surface was procedurally generated.

Rebuild with Node 22: node scripts/prepare-brain-model.mjs [cached-source.glb]
See provenance.json for the source SHA-256 and compression parameters.
Use for educational exploration; this interface does not provide medical localization.
